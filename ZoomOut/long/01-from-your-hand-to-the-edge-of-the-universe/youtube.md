## Description
One continuous zoom, from a hand resting in the grass in Central Park to the edge of the observable universe, 93 billion light-years across. At every step the view gets ten times wider: a person, New York, the Earth, the Moon, the Sun, Voyager 1, the nearest stars, the Milky Way, Andromeda, Laniakea and the cosmic web. Twenty-eight steps of ten in five minutes, then all the way back.

Every distance, size and position is real: the coastlines come from NOAA and US Census data, and the planets, stars and galaxies sit where they really are.
