## Title
What Would Earth Look Like If All the Ice Melted?

## Description
If every glacier and ice sheet on land melted, the sea would rise about 70 m (230 ft), almost to the chest of the Statue of Liberty. In a simple flood-map model, much of Florida, Venice, Amsterdam and Shanghai would go under. But coastlines have been redrawn before, in the other direction.

How much has the sea already risen since 1900? About 20 centimeters, or about 2 meters? Tell me in the comments.

## Real answers (for reference, not for posting)
A, about 20 centimeters. Global mean sea level rose about 0.20 m between 1901 and 2018 (IPCC AR6 WG1 SPM A.1.7: 0.20 m, likely range 0.15-0.25 m), and about 21-23 cm since 1900 counting the years since (NASA). The rate is speeding up: about 1.3 mm a year in 1901-1971, about 3.7 mm a year in 2006-2018 (IPCC AR6). 2 meters would be about ten times the real rise; it is more than even the likely range projected for 2100 under very high emissions (about 0.6-1.0 m, IPCC AR6), and only appears in low-likelihood, high-impact scenarios.
