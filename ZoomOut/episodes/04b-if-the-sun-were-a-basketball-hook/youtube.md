## Title
If the Sun Were a Basketball, Where Would Earth Be?

## Description
Shrink the Sun to a basketball and Earth becomes a peppercorn 26 m (85 ft) away. Neptune sits almost 800 m out, and the whole solar system is mostly empty space. The nearest star is the part that is hard to believe.

Quick guess: at this scale, how far is the center of our galaxy? Across the world, the Moon, or 100× farther? Comment your guess!

Every video starts close and zooms out. Subscribe for a new one every day.

Quick guess 👇 If the Sun were a basketball, how far away would the center of our galaxy be?
A) Across the world

## Answer (post as a reply to the pinned comment after a day or so)
C! The galactic center is about 26,000 light-years away. At this scale (1 : 5.8 billion) that's about 42 million km, roughly 110 times the distance to the Moon.
