# 15 — What If Humans Disappeared?

## Title
What Happens to Our Cities If Humans Suddenly Disappear?

## Description
Within hours the power plants would shut down. Experts estimate New York's subway would flood in about two days, and cracked streets could turn to forest in roughly twenty years. Some of what we made might last for millions of years, but our most lasting trace has already left the planet.
