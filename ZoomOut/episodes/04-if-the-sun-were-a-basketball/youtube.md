# 04 — If the Sun Were a Basketball

## Title
If the Sun Were a Basketball, Where Would Earth Be?

## Description
Shrink the Sun to a basketball and Earth becomes a peppercorn 26 m (85 ft) away. Neptune sits almost 800 m out, and the whole solar system is mostly empty space. The nearest star is the part that is hard to believe.
