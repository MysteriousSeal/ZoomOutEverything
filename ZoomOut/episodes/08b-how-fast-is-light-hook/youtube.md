## Title
How Fast Is Light? Follow One Beam Across the Universe

## Description
Light travels 299,792 km every second (186,282 miles). Follow one beam from the Moon to the Sun, past Neptune and Voyager 1, then on to the nearest star and far beyond. Some of the light you see tonight is very old.

Starlight is a time machine. Which era would you look back at? Tell me in the comments.

Every video starts close and zooms out. Subscribe for a new one every day.

Sources:
BIPM / NIST: speed of light in vacuum, 299,792,458 m/s (exact)
NASA Planetary Fact Sheets: Earth, Moon, Sun and Neptune distances
NASA JPL: Voyager 1 mission status and distance, about 25.9 billion km (late October 2026; one light-day, 25.9 billion km, is crossed around mid-November 2026)
ESA Gaia DR3 / NASA: Proxima Centauri distance, 4.24 light-years
JWST (JADES-GS-z14-0, MoM-z14): light-travel time about 13.5 billion years
