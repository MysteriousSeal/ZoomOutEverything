## Title
What If the Sun Suddenly Became a Black Hole?

## Description
Crush the whole Sun into a black hole and it would be just 6 km (3.7 mi) wide, smaller than a city. For 8 minutes and 20 seconds, nothing would change. Then the sky would go dark, yet Earth would keep the same orbit, 150 million km (93 million mi) out. Within weeks, experts estimate, the land would drop below −18 °C (0 °F), and the twist is why the Sun can never do this.

Crush Earth into a black hole instead. How big would it be? About the size of a marble, or about the size of a car? Tell me in the comments.

## Real answers (for reference, not for posting)
A, about the size of a marble. A black hole's horizon radius is the Schwarzschild radius r = 2GM/c². For Earth's mass (5.972 × 10²⁴ kg, NASA Earth Fact Sheet) r ≈ 8.9 mm, so the horizon would be about 1.8 cm across, marble-sized. Cross-check: the radius scales with mass, and Earth has 1/333,000 of the Sun's mass, so 5.9 km / 333,000 ≈ 1.8 cm. B) A car-sized (~4 m) black hole would need about 225 Earth masses, roughly 0.7 Jupiter masses. (Squeezing Earth that small is hypothetical: nothing in nature could do it.)
