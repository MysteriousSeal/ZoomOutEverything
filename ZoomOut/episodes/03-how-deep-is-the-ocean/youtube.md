# 03 — How Deep Is the Ocean

## Title
How Deep Does the Ocean Really Go?

## Description
Dive from the surface to the bottom of the Mariana Trench, 10,935 m (35,877 ft) down, at true scale. Sunlight fades at 200 m, the deepest scuba diver stops at 332 m, and the Titanic lies at 3,800 m. Then the bottom, and one comparison with Everest.

Every video starts close and zooms out. Subscribe for a new one every day.

Sources:
NOAA Ocean Service: ocean zones and the photic zone (sunlight to about 200 m)
Guinness World Records: deepest scuba dive, 332.35 m, Ahmed Gabr, 2014
Quick et al., 2020: Extreme diving in mammals, Cuvier's beaked whale, 2,992 m
Challenger Deep depth estimates, about 10,920 to 10,994 m
Titanic wreck depth, about 3,800 m (12,500 ft)
