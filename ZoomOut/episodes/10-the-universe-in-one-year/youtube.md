# 10 — The Universe in One Year

## Title
The Entire History of the Universe in One Year

## Description
Squeeze 13.8 billion years into a single calendar year, with the Big Bang at midnight on January 1. Follow the Sun, the first life and the dinosaurs through the months, and watch the final minutes of December 31.

Every video starts close and zooms out. Subscribe for a new one every day.

Sources:
Carl Sagan, 1977: The Dragons of Eden (Cosmic Calendar)
Age of the universe, about 13.8 billion years
Age of the Sun, about 4.57 billion years
Cambrian explosion, about 538 million years ago; K-Pg extinction, 66 million years ago
Homo sapiens, about 300,000 years ago
