# 22 — How Old Is Everything, Really? (hook format, ~71 s)

Series: **Hidden History**. Folder: `ZoomOut/episodes/22-how-old-is-everything/`.

- **Anchor:** "now", a pulsing point at the right end of a single timeline ruler, and one human lifetime (80 years)
- **Ladder:** a lifetime 80 y → the Great Pyramid 4,580 y → Cleopatra (closer to the Moon landing than to the pyramid) → Homo sapiens 300,000 y → the last dinosaurs 66 Myr → T. rex closer to us than to Stegosaurus → sharks (450 Myr) older than trees (385 Myr) → the Earth 4.54 Gyr → the Big Bang 13.8 Gyr

- **Length:** ~71.3 s including the preroll and the question (15 voice lines)
- **Accent colour:** cyan-teal `(120, 210, 230)` · music `deep` · voice af_heart @ 0.92

## FACT-CHECK

All ages are counted back from 2026. There is no year 0, so an event in year N BC lies N + 2026 − 1 years ago.

- **A long life ≈ 80 years.** Life expectancy at birth in high-income countries is about 80 years (OECD average about 80–81), so "a long life" is fair.
- **The Great Pyramid of Giza (Khufu):** completed around 2560 BC (the usual estimates run 2600–2560 BC). 2560 + 2026 − 1 = **4,585 years**, written as ≈ 4,580 and spoken as "about four and a half thousand". ✔
- **Cleopatra VII** died in 30 BC (August).
  - From the pyramid to Cleopatra: 2560 − 30 = **2,530 years**.
  - From Cleopatra to Apollo 11 (July 1969): 30 + 1969 − 1 = **1,998 ≈ 2,000 years**.
  - 2,000 < 2,530, so she lived closer to the Moon landing. ✔ (The claim also holds for the earliest pyramid date: 2600 BC gives a 2,570-year gap.)
- **Homo sapiens ≈ 300,000 years.** The Jebel Irhoud (Morocco) fossils are dated to about 315 ± 34 ka (Richter et al., *Nature* 2017), the oldest known members of our species. ✔
- **The last (non-bird) dinosaurs died 66 million years ago** in the K–Pg extinction, dated to 66.04 ± 0.03 Ma (Chicxulub; Renne et al. 2013; Sprain et al. 2018). ✔
- **T. rex vs Stegosaurus.** *Tyrannosaurus rex* lived about 68–66 Ma, right up to the extinction. *Stegosaurus* lived about 155–150 Ma (Late Jurassic, Morrison Formation).
  - Gap from Stegosaurus to T. rex: at least 150 − 68 = **82 Myr**, typically about 85.
  - Gap from T. rex to us: at most **68 Myr** (≈ 66–68).
  - 82 > 68, so T. rex is closer to us. ✔ The marker sits at 66.5 Ma.
- **Sharks are older than trees.**
  - The oldest shark-like scales date from the Late Ordovician, about **450 Ma** (Sansom et al.; *Elegestolepis* / mongolepid scales). The oldest widely accepted shark teeth are early Devonian, about 410 Ma.
  - The earliest trees: *Wattieza* (Gilboa, NY) about **385–390 Ma** (Middle Devonian); *Archaeopteris*, about 385 Ma, is the first "modern" tree with wood and leaves.
  - 450 > 385. ✔ Even counting only the teeth, 410 > 390.
- **The Earth formed about 4.54 billion years ago**, ± 0.05 Gyr (Dalrymple 2001; meteorite Pb–Pb ages). ✔
- **The universe is 13.8 billion years old**: 13.787 ± 0.020 Gyr (Planck 2018, ΛCDM). ✔

- **A human hair** is 0.05–0.1 mm thick: 0.017–0.18 mm in the extremes, typically 0.07 mm. In track units 0.05 mm = 6,900 years and 0.07 mm ≈ 9,700 years.
- So written history (0.038 mm) is thinner than even a fine 0.05 mm hair. ✔ The film draws a 0.07 mm hair (`HAIR_M`), with the sliver at about half its width.
- **Track geometry** (World Athletics 400 m standard track):
  - lanes 1.22 m wide, lines 50 mm;
  - inner bend radius 36.5 m, straights 84.39 m;
  - the 100 m start is in the straight extension beyond the bend; the finish line is at the end of the home straight;
  - the finish is the edge of the line nearest the start, which is exactly "now".
- **Cards:** "ANCIENT EGYPT" covers the pyramid and Cleopatra (both on screen). The dinosaurs and the Big Bang are on screen too.
