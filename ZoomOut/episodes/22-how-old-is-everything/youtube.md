# 22 — How Old Is Everything? (hook format)

Series: Hidden History. Cold-open hook (written history is thinner than a human hair) and a closing question.

## Title
How Old Is Everything, Really?

## Description
From one human lifetime to the Great Pyramid, the dinosaurs, the birth of the Earth and the Big Bang, on one timeline that keeps zooming out. Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the pyramids. T. rex lived closer to us than to Stegosaurus. Sharks are older than trees. Then we squeeze all 13.8 billion years onto a 100-meter running track. The dinosaurs vanish half a meter from the finish line, our species is the last two millimeters, and all of written history is thinner than a human hair.

If you could visit any moment on that track, where would you go? Tell me in the comments.

Every video starts close and zooms out. Subscribe for a new one every day.
