# 13 — What If the Earth Stopped Spinning?

## Title
What If the Earth Suddenly Stopped Spinning?

## Description
At the equator you are moving at about 1,670 km/h (1,040 mph) right now. Stop the planet suddenly and the air, the oceans and everything not bolted down would keep going, then the map would be redrawn. In the simplest model, a single day would last a whole year, and the strangest part is written in fossils.
