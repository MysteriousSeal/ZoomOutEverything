## Title
What If the Oceans Drained?

## Description
Drain the sea by just 50 meters and you could walk from Alaska to Russia. By 60 meters, Britain has rejoined Europe across Doggerland, and 20,000 years ago the seas really were about 120 meters lower. Keep draining. At 2.5 km the mid-ocean ridge appears: 65,000 km of mountains, the longest range on Earth. At 5 km most of the sea floor is dry, and Mauna Kea stands over 10 km tall from its base. The last water pools in the Mariana Trench, 10,935 meters down. Yet on a 30 cm classroom globe, all of that ocean would be about as thin as a coat of paint. Every frame uses real NOAA bathymetry.

If the seas dropped tonight, where would you walk first? Tell me in the comments.
