## Title
How to Survive a Plane Crash

## Description
Most plane crashes are survivable. What you do in the next ninety seconds decides if you walk away. About 70% of fatal accidents happen during takeoff, climb-out, approach and landing: barely 6% of the flight. So when you sit down, count the rows to your nearest exits, in front and behind. In smoke, you'll find the exit by touch, along the seat backs. Keep your seatbelt low and tight across your hips, and keep sturdy shoes on. If the crew shouts "brace", head down against the seat in front, feet flat on the floor. When the plane stops, leave your bag. Planes are built so everyone can get out in ninety seconds, using only half the exits. Grabbing luggage has cost lives.

Flying is still extremely safe: this is how to be ready, not a reason to be afraid.
