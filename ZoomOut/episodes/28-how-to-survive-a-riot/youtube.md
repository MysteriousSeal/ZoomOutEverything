## Title
How to Survive a Riot

## Description
If a crowd turns into a riot, the biggest danger often isn't the violence. It's the crowd itself: in crowd disasters, most people die because they're crushed so tightly they can't breathe. Step one: leave early. The moment it turns, walk away, and don't stop to film. Stay at the edges of the crowd, near an exit, and avoid walls, fences and narrow streets: that's where people get crushed. If the crowd surges, don't fight it. Go with it, and drift sideways between waves. Arms up, like a boxer, to protect your chest so you can keep breathing. If you fall, get up fast; if you can't, curl up on your side and cover your head. Tear gas? Move upwind and uphill, because it sinks. Don't rub your eyes: flush them with water. Once you're out, get indoors, away from windows.
