## Title
The Deadliest Animal on Earth

## Description
The deadliest animal on Earth isn't a shark. It's not even close. Sharks kill about 6 people a year (ISAF, 10-year average). Snakes kill up to 138,000 (WHO estimate: 81,000–138,000), many of them farm workers far from a hospital. Dogs: about 59,000 a year, almost all from rabies (dogs cause up to 99% of human rabies cases). Humans kill more than 400,000 of each other every year (about 458,000 homicides in 2021). But number one is tiny: the mosquito. It also spreads dengue, Zika and yellow fever, and malaria alone killed about 610,000 people in 2024 — about 95% of them in the WHO African Region, and about three in four of them children under five.
