# Zoom Out: The Deadliest Animal on Earth

**Series:** By the Numbers · **Length:** ≈ 53.2 s · **Voice:** ElevenLabs Brittney, Eleven v4 · **Hook:** surprising claim, readable on frame 1 · **Ending:** comments question

## Narration ("|" = phrase pause)

0. The deadliest animal on Earth isn't a shark. | It's not even close.
1. Sharks kill about six people a year.
2. Snakes kill up to a hundred and thirty-eight thousand. | Many of them farmers, far from a hospital.
3. Dogs: about sixty thousand, | almost all from rabies.
4. Humans kill more than four hundred thousand of each other every year.
5. But number one is tiny. | The mosquito. | It also spreads dengue, Zika and yellow fever.
6. Malaria alone killed six hundred and ten thousand people in 2024, | most of them children under five.
7. Which animal scares you the most? | Tell me in the comments.

## FACT-CHECK (per year, worldwide)

- **Sharks:** ISAF (Florida Museum, International Shark Attack File): about 6 unprovoked fatal bites per year on average over the last decade (2025: 65 unprovoked bites, 9–12 deaths depending on classification). "About six a year": OK. On screen: "≈ 6".
- **Snakes:** WHO: 1.8–2.7 million envenomings per year, **81,410–137,880 deaths**; most victims are agricultural workers and children in rural areas of Africa, Asia and Latin America, often far from health care. On screen: "UP TO 138,000" (or "81,000–138,000").
- **Dogs:** WHO: rabies kills **≈ 59,000** people a year; dogs cause up to 99% of human rabies cases. "About sixty thousand, almost all from rabies": OK. On screen "≈ 59,000".
- **Humans:** UNODC Global Study on Homicide (2023): **≈ 458,000** intentional homicides in 2021 (≈ 440,000 in other recent years). "More than four hundred thousand": OK. On screen "≈ 450,000" or "400,000+". Show humans as a neutral silhouette icon or number only: no violence.
- **Mosquito:** WHO World Malaria Report 2025: **610,000 malaria deaths in 2024**, 282 million cases; ≈ 95% in the WHO African Region; ≈ 75% of those deaths are children under 5. Mosquitoes also transmit dengue, Zika, yellow fever (WHO). On screen: "610,000 · MALARIA ALONE · 2024".
- **Scale note:** a bar/area chart must be honest (true proportions; sharks' 6 will be invisible next to 610,000: show it with a magnifier/inset, never fake the scale).
- Avoid: gore, attack imagery, crude animals. Animals as refined, realistic or elegant illustrated silhouettes (consistent style), never cartoon faces.
