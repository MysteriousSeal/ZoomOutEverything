# 16 — What If the Sun Became a Black Hole?

## Title
What Would Happen If the Sun Turned Into a Black Hole?

## Description
For 8 minutes and 20 seconds, nothing would change. Then the sky would go dark, yet Earth would keep the same orbit, 150 million km (93 million mi) out. The whole Sun would fit in a ball about 6 km (3.7 mi) wide, and the twist is why it will never happen.
