## Real answers (for reference, not for posting)
B, it rotated. The Soviet "nepreryvka" (continuous week), brought in from 1 October 1929, split each workforce into five groups, each marked by a colour (commonly yellow, pink/peach, red, purple, green) or a Roman numeral I-V, with staggered rest days. About 80% of workers were on the job on any given day and 20% were resting, so factories never stopped, and there was no common day off: husbands, wives and friends often had different rest days. Stalin condemned it in June 1931, and most industry moved from late 1931 to a six-day week with a shared rest day (the 6th, 12th, 18th, 24th and 30th of each month). The seven-day week returned by a decree of 26 June 1940. The film's 5-day calendar page is drawn plain in this version, so the answer never appears in the film.
