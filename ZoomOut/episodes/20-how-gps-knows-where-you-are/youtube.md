# 20 — How GPS Knows Where You Are

## Title
How Does Your Phone Know Exactly Where You Are?

## Description
About 31 satellites circle some 20,000 km (12,400 mi) overhead, and your phone never sends them a thing. It only listens, and timing must be good to a billionth of a second. The strangest part of the answer involves Einstein.
