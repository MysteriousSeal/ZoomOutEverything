## Title
How Many Ants Are on Earth?

## Description
Start with one ant, then one colony under a stone, then a leafcutter nest with millions. In 2022, scientists combined 489 ground surveys and arrived at a conservative estimate: 20 quadrillion ants. That's nearly 2.5 million for every person alive, and together they outweigh all wild birds and wild mammals combined. Line them up at 5 mm each and the line would reach about 670 times the distance from Earth to the Sun, far past Pluto.
