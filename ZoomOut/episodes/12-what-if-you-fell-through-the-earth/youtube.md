# 12 — What If You Fell Through the Earth?

## Title
What If You Fell Straight Through the Center of the Earth?

## Description
Crust, mantle, a sea of liquid iron, then a core as hot as the surface of the Sun. In this thought experiment you fall through the planet at up to about 8 km/s (5 mi/s), with no air to slow you. In the simplest model the whole trip takes the same time between any two points on Earth, and the number may surprise you.
