## Title
What If Humans Disappeared Tomorrow?

## Description
Within hours the power plants would shut down. Experts estimate New York's subway would flood in about 2 days, and within roughly 20 years cracked streets would start turning to forest. Some of what we made might last for millions of years, but our longest-lasting trace has already left the planet: about a century of radio and TV broadcasts, now about 100 light-years out in every direction.

How many stars have our signals already reached? About 100, or over 10,000? Tell me in the comments.
