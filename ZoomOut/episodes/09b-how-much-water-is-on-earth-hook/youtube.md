# 09b — How Much Water Is on Earth (hook version)

Alternative cut of episode 09: a cold-open hook (all of Earth's water fits in a ball smaller than the Moon) and a closing question. The original stays in ../09-how-much-water-is-on-earth/.

## Title
All of Earth's Water in One Ball: How Big Is It?

## Description
Gather every drop of water on Earth into a single sphere and measure it against the planet, and against the Moon. Then keep only the liquid fresh water, then only the fresh-water lakes and rivers. The last ball is smaller than you would guess.

Did you think there was more water? Way more, about this, or less? Tell me in the comments.
