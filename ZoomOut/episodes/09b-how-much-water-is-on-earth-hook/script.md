## Fact-check notes

Source: USGS Water Science School, "How Much Water is There on Earth?" (Perlman, Cook & Nieman).
- All water ≈ 1,386 million km³ → sphere diameter ≈ 1,385 km.
- Liquid fresh water (groundwater, swamps, rivers, lakes) ≈ 10.6 million km³ → ≈ 273 km sphere. USGS excludes ice from this sphere, which is why line 8 says "liquid fresh water" and doesn't list ice.
- Lakes and rivers ≈ 93,100 km³ → ≈ 56 km sphere.
- Moon diameter 3,474 km; 1,385 / 3,474 ≈ 0.40, "less than half".
- 71% ocean coverage (NOAA/USGS).
- Greater London is ~50–60 km across, so "roughly the width of London" fits a 56 km sphere.
