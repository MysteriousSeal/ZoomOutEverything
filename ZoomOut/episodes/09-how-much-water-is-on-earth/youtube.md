# 09 — How Much Water Is on Earth

## Title
All of Earth's Water in One Ball: How Big Is It?

## Description
Gather every drop of water on Earth into a single sphere and measure it against the planet, and against the Moon. Then keep only the fresh water, then only the lakes and rivers. The last ball is smaller than you would guess.
