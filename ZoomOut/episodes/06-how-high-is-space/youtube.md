# 06 — How High Is Space

## Title
How Far Up Is Space, Really?

## Description
Climb from the Statue of Liberty past Everest and the jets, all the way to the Kármán line, 100 km (62 mi) up. Along the way, 99 percent of the air disappears and a skydiver breaks the sound barrier. Then we check where the Space Station sits on a classroom globe.
