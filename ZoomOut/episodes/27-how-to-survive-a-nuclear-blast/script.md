## FACT-CHECK (official US guidance: Ready.gov / FEMA "Get inside, stay inside, stay tuned")

- **Time to shelter:** outside the immediate blast zone, fallout often takes more than 15 minutes to reach ground level; Ready.gov: "you will have 10 minutes or more to find an adequate shelter". "About ten minutes" is the conservative figure.
- **The flash:** if outdoors, don't look at the flash or fireball (it can blind); lie face down, protect exposed skin and your head (hands under the body), and stay down until the heat and blast wave have passed.
- **The blast wave:** arrives seconds after the flash, depending on distance (it travels at roughly the speed of sound or faster near the burst: about 5 s per 1.6 km / 1 mile further out). Flying glass and debris are the main danger; stay away from windows.
- **Shelter:** get inside the nearest building, preferably **brick or concrete**; go to the **basement** or the **middle** of the building, away from outer walls and the roof. Mass and distance from the outside reduce exposure (FEMA "protection factor": a basement or the core of a large building gives far more protection than a car or a wooden house). Don't shelter in a car.
- **Fallout:** radioactive dust and debris sucked up by the fireball that falls back over minutes to hours, downwind. Most dangerous in the first hours.
- **The 7-10 rule:** for every 7-fold increase in time after the detonation, the dose rate drops about 10-fold: after **7 hours ≈ 10%** (ten times weaker), after **49 hours (≈ 2 days) ≈ 1%** (a hundred times weaker).
- **Stay inside:** at least **24 hours** unless officials say otherwise; listen to a battery or hand-crank radio. If you were outside when fallout arrived: remove the outer layer of clothing and wash skin and hair.
- Avoid: "you'll be safe", fake precise distances, cartoon mushroom-cloud gore, real cities as targets.
- **Sources:** Ready.gov "Nuclear Explosion" (FEMA); FEMA/NUSTL "Planning Guidance for Response to a Nuclear Detonation" (7-10 rule, shelter protection factors).
- **Added lines (60 s version):** the flash can cause temporary or permanent blindness. Flying glass and debris cause many blast injuries: stay away from windows. Cars give very little protection from fallout (FEMA). Ready.gov: stay where you are; don't travel to reunite with family during the fallout period, they should shelter where they are.
