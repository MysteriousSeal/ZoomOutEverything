## Title
How to Survive a Nuclear Blast

## Description
If a nuclear bomb goes off near you, you have about ten minutes to save your life. Here's exactly what to do, according to official guidance. First, the flash: don't look, it can blind you. Drop face down and cover your head. Seconds later the blast wave hits: stay down until it passes, then stay away from windows, because flying glass kills. Then get inside: brick or concrete, in the basement or the middle of the building. Not your car, it barely protects you. Because the fallout is coming: radioactive dust, deadliest in the first hours. After seven hours it's about ten times weaker; after two days, a hundred times. So stay inside for at least 24 hours, unless officials tell you otherwise, and keep a radio on. Don't go looking for family: they should shelter where they are. If you were outside when the dust fell, take off your outer clothes and wash.
