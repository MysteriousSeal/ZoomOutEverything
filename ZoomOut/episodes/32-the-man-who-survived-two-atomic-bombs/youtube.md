## Title
The Man Who Survived Two Atomic Bombs

## Description
He survived the atomic bomb on Hiroshima. Three days later, he was in Nagasaki. On 6 August 1945, Tsutomu Yamaguchi, a 29-year-old Mitsubishi engineer from Nagasaki, was in Hiroshima on a business trip when the bomb exploded at 8:15 a.m., about 3 km away. His eardrums ruptured, the upper left side of his body was burned and he was temporarily blinded, but he survived. The next day he took the train home to Nagasaki. On 9 August, bandaged, he went back to work, and he was telling his supervisor about the Hiroshima flash when, at 11:02 a.m., the second bomb exploded, again about 3 km away. He survived that one too. Japan only officially recognised him as a survivor of both bombings (a "nijū hibakusha", 二重被爆者) in 2009. He died in 2010, aged 93. More than 160 people lived through both bombings; he is the only one the government officially recognised.
