# 18 — Every Second on the Internet

## Title
What Happens on the Internet in One Single Second?

## Description
In one second people type more than 100,000 searches into Google and send about 4 million emails. Behind it all sit data centers that experts estimate use roughly 1.5% of the world's electricity, and about 1.4 million km (870,000 mi) of cable on the sea floor. One of these numbers is stranger than the rest.
