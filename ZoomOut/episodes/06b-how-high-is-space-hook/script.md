## Fact-check notes

- Statue of Liberty: 93 m from ground to torch (including pedestal). Eiffel Tower: 330 m (with antennas, since 2022). Burj Khalifa: 828 m. Everest: 8,849 m (2020 survey).
- Commercial jets cruise at ~10–12 km (33,000–39,000 ft).
- About 99% of the atmosphere's mass lies below ~30–31 km.
- Felix Baumgartner, 14 Oct 2012: jumped from ~39 km (128,100 ft) and reached Mach ~1.25 in free fall. (Alan Eustace jumped from ~41 km in 2014, hence "a skydiver *once*" rather than "the record".)
- Kármán line: 100 km, used by the FAI as the boundary of space (NASA/USAF use ~80 km; worth a pinned reply if asked).
- 100 km at 100 km/h = 1 hour.
- ISS altitude ~400–420 km.
- Classroom globe 30 cm (radius 15 cm): 400 / 6,371 × 15 cm ≈ 0.94 cm.
- Pinned comment: Moon ≈ 384,400 km ÷ 100 km/h ≈ 3,844 h ≈ 160 days.
