# 01 — A Billion Dollars in Cash

## Title
What a Billion Dollars Actually Looks Like

## Description
A million dollars in hundred-dollar bills weighs 10 kg (22 lb) and fits in a briefcase. A billion is ten pallets, ten tonnes of cash. Then we go one step further, and the scale stops making sense.
