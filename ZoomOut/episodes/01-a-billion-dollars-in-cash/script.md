## Fact-check notes

- **$100 bill:** 156 × 66.3 mm, ~0.11 mm thick (0.0043 in), weighs ~1 g (US BEP figures).
- **$10,000** = 100 bills ≈ 1.1 cm thick.
- **$1M** = 10,000 bills ≈ 10 kg; volume ≈ 156 × 66.3 × 1,100 mm ≈ 11.4 L, which fits in a large briefcase.
- **$100M** = 1M bills ≈ 1.14 m³, which matches a standard pallet stacked ~1 m high (≈ 1.2 m × 1.0 m footprint).
- **$1B** = 10 such pallets ≈ 10,000 kg = 10 t.
- **$1T** = 10,000 pallets ≈ 12,000 m² at single height, so ≈ 6,000 m² stacked two high. An American football field including end zones is ≈ 5,350 m², so "about a football field" is fair.
- **Richest people:** the top fortunes have been in the hundreds of billions (Forbes/Bloomberg). Check current figures before publishing.
