## Title
The Man Who Saved the World

## Description
26 September 1983, just after midnight, in a secret bunker near Moscow. The Soviet early-warning computer reports a US missile launch. Then another. Five in total, rated "high reliability". Three weeks earlier the USSR had shot down Korean Air Lines Flight 007, and Cold War tension was at its peak. The duty officer, Lieutenant Colonel Stanislav Petrov, had minutes to report it, and a report could have triggered a counterstrike. But a real American first strike would have meant hundreds of missiles, not five, and the ground radars saw nothing. He called it a false alarm, and waited. Twenty-three minutes later, nothing had happened. He was right: sunlight glinting off high clouds had fooled the satellite. The story stayed secret until 1998.
