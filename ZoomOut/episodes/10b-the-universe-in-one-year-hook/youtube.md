## Real answers (for reference, not for posting)
A. The Great Pyramid of Giza is about 4,600 years old (c. 2560 BC). One calendar second is 13.8 billion ÷ 31,536,000 ≈ 437.6 years, so 4,600 years ≈ 10.5 seconds before midnight (about 11:59:49.5 PM on Dec 31). Ten minutes before midnight would be about 263,000 years ago, before most of the Homo sapiens story (humans arrive at about 11:48 PM). Recorded history: writing about 5,200 years ago ≈ 11.9 s; Columbus, 1492 ≈ 1.2 s.
