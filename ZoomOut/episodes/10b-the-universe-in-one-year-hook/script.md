## Fact-check notes

Scale: 13.8 billion years ÷ 365 days ≈ **37.8 million years per day**, and 13.8 × 10⁹ ÷ 31,536,000 s ≈ **437.6 years per second**.
- Sun formed ≈ 4.57 Gya → day 365 × (1 − 4.57/13.8) ≈ 244 → **Sept 1–2**.
- Earliest evidence of life ≈ 3.7–4.0 Gya → **Sept 15–23** ("late September").
- Cambrian explosion ≈ 538 Mya → Dec 17 (hence "for months, almost nothing but microbes").
- First dinosaurs ≈ 230–245 Mya → **Dec 24–25** ("around Christmas").
- K–Pg extinction 66 Mya → Dec 30 (~06:30).
- Homo sapiens ≈ 300,000 years → 300,000 / 437.6 ≈ 686 s ≈ 11.4 min before midnight → **≈ 11:48 pm**.
- Writing ≈ 5,200 years → 5,200 / 437.6 ≈ 11.9 s → "the last twelve seconds".
- Columbus 1492 → 534 years → 1.22 s → "just over one second".
- An 80-year life → 80 / 437.6 ≈ 0.18 s → "less than a fifth of a second".
- Concept: Carl Sagan, *The Dragons of Eden* (1977), Cosmic Calendar; figures updated to current estimates.
