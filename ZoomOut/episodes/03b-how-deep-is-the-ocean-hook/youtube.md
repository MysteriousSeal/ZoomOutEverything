Alternative cut of episode 03: a cold-open hook (Everest sinking into the Mariana Trench) and a closing question. The original stays in ../03-how-deep-is-the-ocean/.

## Title
Everest Would Vanish in the Deepest Ocean

Alternative: How Deep Does the Ocean Really Go?

## Description
Drop Everest into the ocean's deepest point and it disappears, with 2 km of water still above its summit. Dive from the surface to the bottom of the Mariana Trench, 10,935 m (35,877 ft) down, at true scale. Sunlight fades at 200 m, the deepest scuba diver stops at 332 m, and the Titanic lies at 3,800 m. Then the bottom, and one comparison with Everest.

How deep would you go: scuba, the Titanic, or the very bottom? Tell me in the comments.

Sources:
NOAA Ocean Service: ocean zones and the photic zone (sunlight to about 200 m)
Guinness World Records: deepest scuba dive, 332.35 m, Ahmed Gabr, 2014
Quick et al., 2020: Extreme diving in mammals, Cuvier's beaked whale, 2,992 m
Challenger Deep depth estimates, about 10,920 to 10,994 m
Titanic wreck depth, about 3,800 m (12,500 ft)
