## Narration script

> Could every human on Earth fit in one place?
> There are about eight billion of us.
> Give everyone a square meter, | and we'd cover an area the size of Puerto Rico.
> Pack in like a concert crowd, four per square meter, | and we'd all fit inside Rhode Island, with room to spare.
> Now pack everyone, by volume, into a single cube.
> It's about eight hundred meters tall. | Shorter than the Burj Khalifa.
> Drop it into the Grand Canyon, and you'd barely find it.
> Spread us evenly over the whole planet, | and we'd be a film seventy times thinner than a human hair.
> Physically, humanity is almost nothing.
> But there's one more number.
> About a hundred and seventeen billion humans have ever lived.
> Everyone alive today is just seven percent of them.
> For every one of us, | about thirteen people have already come and gone.

## Fact-check notes

- **Population:** ~8.2 billion (UN World Population Prospects 2024). Check the latest estimate before publishing.
- **1 m² each:** 8.2 × 10⁹ m² = 8,200 km². Puerto Rico ≈ 9,104 km² (the main island ≈ 8,900 km²), so "the size of Puerto Rico" is fair (slightly smaller). A square of side ≈ 90.5 km.
- **4 per m²** (a dense concert crowd) → ≈ 2,050 km². Rhode Island ≈ 3,144 km² total (≈ 2,700 km² of land), so everyone fits with room to spare. Tokyo was dropped: its official 2,194 km² includes the remote Izu and Ogasawara islands, and the mainland part (~1,800 km²) is smaller than the crowd.
- **Volume:** average body mass ~62 kg at about water density → ~0.062 m³ per person. 8.2 × 10⁹ × 0.062 ≈ 5.1 × 10⁸ m³ ≈ 0.5 km³, so the cube side ≈ 800 m. The Burj Khalifa is 828 m. Note: 62 kg is the adult mean; the all-ages mean (≈ 50 kg) gives ≈ 740 m, and the claim (shorter than the Burj Khalifa) still holds.
- **Grand Canyon:** ~446 km long, up to ~1.8 km deep, volume ≈ 4,000+ km³. The cube is ~0.01% of it.
- **Film:** 5.1 × 10⁸ m³ ÷ Earth's surface 5.1 × 10¹⁴ m² ≈ 1 × 10⁻⁶ m = **1 µm**. A human hair is ~17–180 µm (typically ~70 µm), hence "about seventy times thinner".
- **Humans ever born:** ~117 billion (Population Reference Bureau estimate, 2022). It's an estimate with wide uncertainty, so say "about".
- **7%:** 8.2 ÷ 117 ≈ 7.0%.
- **13 before you:** (117 − 8.2) ÷ 8.2 ≈ 13.3.
- Phrase the cube as "by volume" so it reads as maths rather than anything gruesome.
