## Title
Could All 8 Billion of Us Fit in One Place?

## Description
Give all ~8.2 billion people one square meter each and we would cover about 8,200 km² (3,200 sq mi). Pack us tightly and we shrink fast. Then one more number puts humanity in a different light.

If you could meet one of the 117 billion people who have ever lived, who would it be? Tell me in the comments.
