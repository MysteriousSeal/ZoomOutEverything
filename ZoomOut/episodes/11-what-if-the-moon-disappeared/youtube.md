# 11 — What If the Moon Disappeared?

## Title
What Really Happens If the Moon Suddenly Disappears?

## Description
No Moon would mean nights about 100 times darker, tides a third of today's biggest and no more total solar eclipses. Many models also say Earth's tilt could start to wobble over millions of years, with seasons to match. And the real twist is what the Moon is already doing.
