## 2. Banner (channel art)

```
Minimalist flat vector YouTube channel banner for a science and curiosity channel called "ZOOM OUT", tagline "Let's zoom out."

Canvas: wide 16:9 landscape image.

MOST IMPORTANT, the layout: this banner gets cropped very hard on phones. Only a thin horizontal strip in the exact centre stays visible (60% of the width, 29% of the height). So ALL text and the logo must be SMALL and grouped tightly in the middle of the image:
- the whole logo + text group must fit in a box only about 45% of the image width and 18% of the image height, perfectly centred;
- leave big empty margins: nothing important in the top 38%, the bottom 38%, the left 27% or the right 27% of the image;
- the text must NOT touch or run near any edge; it should look almost too small, floating in the middle of a large calm navy field.

Design: solid navy background (#0B1424) filling the whole canvas. In the centre, in one horizontal row: on the left a small logo mark, on the right the words "ZOOM OUT" in a bold, heavy, condensed all-caps sans-serif in white, with the tagline "Let's zoom out." directly below it, smaller, in cyan (#5ADCEB). Logo mark: 3 plain concentric circle outlines of increasing size that all touch at one point at their lower-left, like circles expanding up and to the right (a zoom-out), with a small solid cyan dot inside the smallest circle. Inner circle cyan, outer circles white. The mark is NOT a target, NOT a crosshair, NOT a scope: no tick marks, no cross lines, no notches. Background decoration: a few very large, very thin concentric circle outlines in a slightly lighter navy (#16233A), spreading out from the logo across the whole canvas, very subtle.

Style: flat 2D vector, solid colours only, lots of negative space, no gradients, no shadows, no glow, no 3D, no textures, no photos, no planets, no cities, no ruler, no extra text. Spell the text exactly as given. Clean, modern, premium.
```

**Tips:**
- **Layout:** YouTube only shows the centre strip, 1546×423 of the 2560×1440 image, on every device. Image generators tend to make the text huge, so the prompt asks for the logo and text to fill only about 45% × 18% of the image. If they still come out too big, reply: "Make the logo and text 40% smaller, keep everything else identical."
- **Size:** ChatGPT exports about 1536×1024 or 1792×1024, which is under YouTube's minimum of 2560×1440 and gives you that warning. Before uploading:
  1. Crop it to 16:9.
  2. Upscale it to exactly 2560×1440, in Canva (Resize) or Photoshop.
  3. Or send me the file and I'll crop and upscale it.
- **Text:** if it comes out misspelled, generate the banner **without text** (add "no text at all" to the prompt) and type "ZOOM OUT" yourself in Canva or Figma with Bebas Neue.
- **Check:** use YouTube's banner preview (desktop, mobile, TV) before saving.
- Use the same ring mark in the avatar and the banner, so the channel reads as one brand.
- Because the design is geometric, I can also draw both as true SVG files directly (exact colours, crisp at any size). Just ask.
